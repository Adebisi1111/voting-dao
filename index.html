FAQ

What is VotingDAO?

VotingDAO is a decentralized governance platform built on GenLayer. Stake 0.1 GEN to vote on proposals, with all results verified by AI consensus.

Why do I need to stake?

Staking 0.1 GEN prevents spam and ensures only committed participants can vote. You can unstake after your votes are finalized.

How does AI consensus work?

Validators independently verify the final result by re-running the tally from on-chain data. They only agree if the final verdict matches, ensuring no manipulation.

What chain is this on?

VotingDAO is deployed on GenLayer Bradbury Testnet. You can verify all transactions on the Bradbury Explorer.
