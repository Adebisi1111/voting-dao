VotingDAO is a gasless governance platform built on GenLayer. Create proposals, cast votes, and let AI consensus tally the results — all without paying gas fees. Transparent, immutable, and fair.

How It Works

📝

Create Proposal

Submit a proposal with title, description, and deadline. Anyone can create.

🗳️

Cast Votes

Register and vote yes or no. One address, one vote per proposal.

🤖

AI Consensus

Validators verify the final result. No manipulation possible.

✅

Finalize

Results are permanent and verifiable on-chain. No disputes.

Why VotingDAO

⛽

Gasless

No gas fees for voting. Backend relay handles transactions.

🔒

Secure

One address, one vote. Double-voting impossible.

🤖

AI Verified

Consensus ensures all validators agree on the final result.

📊

Transparent

All votes and results verifiable on GenLayer Bradbury.
